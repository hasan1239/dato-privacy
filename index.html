Dato Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy covers the Dato Android app (package app.hasan.dates), made by its developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at hasantariq.apps@gmail.com.

In short

Dato keeps your dates on your phone. It has no account, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. We never receive your data.

What the app stores, and where

Dato stores what you enter: your things (for example "Car"), the dates you track, each time you log one, and any notes and costs you add. It also stores your settings, such as the theme, language and reminder time.

All of this is kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage. No other app can read it.

What is collected or shared

Nothing. Dato does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code, and it sends nothing to any server of its own.

When data leaves your phone

Only in ways you control:

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Dato's dates and settings in your Google backup, and in a transfer to a new phone. Google handles this backup under its own terms. You can turn it off in system settings at any time.
  • Backup files. Settings → Back up → Export backup saves a file wherever you choose. The file is not encrypted: anyone who has it can read your dates, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Spreadsheet export. Settings → Export as CSV saves your history as a spreadsheet file wherever you choose.

Dato never sends these files anywhere by itself.

Purchases

Dato Pro is a one-off purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro.

The Google Play Billing library that makes this possible is supplied by Google and may send its own diagnostic information to Google. This is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may collect crash and "app not responding" reports and show them, without your data, to the developer in Google Play Console. The app contains no code for this; it is part of Android and Google Play, and you control it in your phone's settings.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are scheduled and shown by your phone. Home screen widgets read your dates on the phone. Neither sends anything anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders, and only if you allow them.
  • Run at start-up: to set reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing and network access: used by Google Play for the Pro purchase. Dato's own code makes no network requests.

Deleting your data

Delete any date or thing in the app, or uninstall Dato to remove everything it stores on your phone. Copies you exported, and Android backups, are yours to delete in the places you keep them.

Children

Dato is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new "last updated" date.
